common_passwords.txt — bundled breached/common-password blocklist
==================================================================

Source : SecLists — Passwords/Common-Credentials/10k-most-common.txt
         https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists
         (the 10,000 most common passwords from public breach corpora)
Retrieved verbatim; 10,000 entries, one password per line.

License: MIT License — Copyright (c) 2018 Daniel Miessler and contributors.
         Redistributable; see the SecLists LICENSE. This file ships unchanged
         under those terms as ENGINE data (it is not house content).

Purpose: Heresay rejects any of these at every point a password is SET
         (account creation, .passwd change, recovery reset). It is a
         frequency list of what attackers try first, so by its nature it
         contains offensive and explicit strings — that is the point of the
         list, not an endorsement of its contents.
