.unban — lift a ban early (staff)

  .unban <name|ip>   entry is allowed again

Bans expire by themselves, so this is for ending one ahead of time. If
nothing was banned under that name or address it says so. Logged as a fact.

Lifting is gated like placing: you can only unban someone BELOW your own
rank, so a ban placed from above you is not yours to undo.
