.promote — lift someone one rung (owner)

  .promote <name>   one rank up, persisted

They gain the new rank at once if online, and keep it across restarts.

Two limits, and they are the point. The person must be BELOW your own rank
— you cannot promote a peer, or yourself. And the result may reach your own
rank but never pass it. The owner is named in the server config, not
granted here, so this can never make another owner.

Logged as a fact. .demote is the way back.
