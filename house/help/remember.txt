remember — stay logged in on this device (browser only)

  .remember on|off     turn it on, or off and sign out every device

Off unless you turn it on. With it on, a browser logs you back in
without your password for 30 days; each visit renews that, 30 unused
days ends it.

To stay logged in, just close or background the tab. It has to work
that way: a phone throws away a backgrounded tab to save memory, and
a thrown-away tab looks exactly like a fresh one when you return.
Nothing can tell "I closed it" from "the phone tidied up" — so
without this, glancing at a text costs you your password.

To log out, use .quit. That signs you out on every device, not just
this one, and you type your password next time. It does NOT turn this
setting off, so that next login remembers you again. Use .remember
off to stop being remembered at all.

What's stored is an opaque token, not your password, and it cannot
become one. Changing your password also signs out every device.

On a borrowed or shared device, leave it off, or .quit when done.
