security — a glance at the locks

  .security             counts over the last day, and nothing else

Six numbers: failed login attempts, login timeouts, password resets
made with a recovery code, recovery code sets regenerated, session
takeovers, and accounts their owners deleted.

Counts only. No names, no addresses, no times — the screen answers
"is anything happening?", and when the answer is yes the server log
is where the answer to "who, and from where?" lives. That split is
deliberate: a number tells you to go and look, and looking is a thing
someone should have to decide to do.

The window is the house's security_window_hours (a day by default).
It can never reach further back than the event log itself keeps, so a
larger setting simply shows everything still held.

Re-consenting to a revised agreement is not counted. It happens to
everyone at once whenever the agreement changes, and a row that says
"the whole house" tells you nothing about anybody.

owner only.
