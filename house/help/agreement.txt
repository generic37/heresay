The text you were shown when you made your account, laid out for the
screen you are reading it on. Nothing is asked and nothing happens:
it is the agreement, not the prompt.

It is the CURRENT text, and that is the same thing as the text you
agreed to. If it is ever revised you are asked again at your next
login, so what stands here is always what stands between you and the
house.

Two things it is worth re-reading it for: what the house keeps (very
little, and each exception named), and the honest limit on the
encryption — the mail and private lines it holds are encrypted at
rest, not end to end, so whoever runs the server could read what the
server holds.

Everyone can read it, at any rank.
