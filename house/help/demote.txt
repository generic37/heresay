.demote — lower someone one rung (owner)

  .demote <name>   one rank down, persisted

Takes the rank away at once if they are online. Stops at the bottom of the
ladder — there is nothing below it.

You can only demote someone BELOW your own rank: not a peer, not anyone
above you. The owner's own rank comes from the server config and cannot
be demoted from in here.

Logged as a fact.
