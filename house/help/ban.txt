.ban — refuse an account entry (staff)

  .ban <name>          24 hours (the default)
  .ban <name> <hours>  a specific length

Bans the ACCOUNT. For the address someone is connecting from, see
.ban_ip — they were one command until v1.32.0, and banning a person and
banning an address are different enough to be asked for separately.

You can only ban someone BELOW your own rank. Not a peer, not anyone
above you, and never the owner.

Persists across restarts and expires on its own. Deleting an account does
NOT lift a ban on its name — deletion must not be a way around one.
Recorded as a fact.
