.ban_ip — ban the address someone is on (owner)

  .ban_ip <name>          24 hours (the default)
  .ban_ip <name> <hours>  a specific length

You name a PERSON and the house finds the address: their live session if
they are connected, otherwise the last one they logged in from, if that
was recent enough to still be remembered. If they are offline and have
not been seen in a while there is no address to ban, and you are told so
— nothing about where people connect from is kept any longer than the
rest of the operational record.

An address is not a person. It can be a house, a flat share, a college
block, or a whole phone network. So before it does anything it tells you
how many accounts have been seen on that address and which of them are
on it now, and asks. Anything but a plain y leaves it alone.

You can only do this to someone BELOW your own rank, and an address that
carries staff is not yours to ban at all: if the ban would reach any
staff account, you are told that much and no more, and it stops there.
Only the owner can go on — and only after being told which staff it
would catch, and saying so again.

The owner's address is never refused, and the local console never is
either — the house cannot be locked away from the person who keeps it.
