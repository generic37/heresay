.logins — open or close new logons (owner)

  .logins        show the current setting
  .logins off    nobody new may log in
  .logins on     open again

The owner can always get in, so this cannot lock you out of your own
house. Sessions already connected are untouched; it refuses NEW logons.
Persists across restarts. Pair with .logoutall to empty the place.
