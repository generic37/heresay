.examine — look closer

.examine <name> shows user description and profile, online or not.
See .desc and .profile. Users indicated as staff may be contacted
for assistance either directly or via .call_staff <text> / .mail_staff.

.examine on its own examines YOU — the same screen anyone else gets
when they look you up, marking and all. Worth a glance after setting
a .desc or a .profile, to see how it reads to a stranger.

Shortcuts are: .ex .exa
