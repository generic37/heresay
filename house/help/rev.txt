.rev — what the room still remembers of the last half hour

Says and emotes only; thinks, shouts, tells and notices are not kept.
Works only while standing in the entry room; oldest first.

It replays the room as you would have seen it live: people you have
.muted are left out, and so is anything you have .ignored — if the
room's murmurs are off for you, they stay off in the replay.

Never on disk; a restart empties it, an empty room does not. Side
rooms remember nothing — for privacy from later arrivals, speak
somewhere other than the entry room.
