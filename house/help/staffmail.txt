.staffmail — read the shared staff letterbox (staff)

  .staffmail   every note residents have left for staff

One box, shared by all staff — .mail_staff is how residents write to it.
Numbered, with sender and date, so .dstaffmail can clear one for everyone
once it is handled. It is ordinary mail underneath: encrypted at rest,
capped, and purged after 180 days.

Staff are told "You have staff mail." at login, and nowhere else. Unlike
personal mail, that line fires whenever the box is not empty: the box is
shared, so one person reading it cannot mean everyone has. Clear a handled
note with .dstaffmail and it stops for all of you.
