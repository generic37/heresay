.free — let someone out of jail (staff)

  .free <name>    back to the entry room, flag cleared

They are shown the house's release line (.ownerset release_msg) and the
room they land in. Clears the persistent status, so a relog no longer puts
them back. With no jail room configured this says so and does nothing.
Logged as a fact.

You can only free someone BELOW your own rank — a release decided above you
is not yours to undo — and you cannot free yourself. The owner is the
one exception: they can free themselves, so that a jailed owner is never
locked in.
