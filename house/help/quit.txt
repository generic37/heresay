.quit — leave the talker

Announces your going (your .set logout_msg) and closes the
connection. What you said stays only in the memories of those
present (or in the entry room's fading .rev buffer).
Shortcuts are: .q .x

It is also a full logout: if you had .remember on, quitting signs
you out on every device and you type your password next time. The
setting stays on, so that login remembers you again. To stay logged
in, close the tab instead. (.help remember)
