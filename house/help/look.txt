.look — where am I?

Re-shows the room: name, description, present company, exits, and the
topic if one is set. The same view you got on arrival or entry to the
room. Shortcut: .l

"Present company" lists everyone in the room, yourself included, in
alphabetical order — each name followed by that person's description
(.desc sets your own). Rank is not shown here; .examine <name> tells
you what rank someone holds.
