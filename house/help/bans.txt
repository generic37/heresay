.bans — what is currently banned (staff)

Lists every ban in force: account bans under the account's name, and
address bans under the name of whoever they were placed on. Who set it,
why, and when it runs out.

Expired bans are not listed. They lift themselves, and a ban that has
ended is not news.

No addresses appear here. An address ban reads as the person it was
placed on, which is how it was placed and so how it reads back — the
house resolves addresses, it does not show them.
