staff_calls — who has been reaching for staff

  .staff_calls          a tally over the last day, busiest first

Two doors lead to the staff: .call_staff, which interrupts everyone
awake, and .mail_staff, which waits in the shared letterbox. This
counts both, one line per resident, so someone who has called eleven
times since breakfast is the first name on the screen.

Names and counts, nothing else. Not what anyone said — a call is
never written down, and a note is mail, read by the people it was
addressed to. Not when, and not where from.

The window is the house's staff_calls_window_hours (a day by
default), and can never reach further back than the event log keeps.

A quiet day shows one line saying so. That is the good outcome.

Staff and the owner.
