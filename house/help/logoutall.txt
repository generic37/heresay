.logoutall — close the house (owner)

  .logoutall   confirms first, then drops every session but the owner's

For maintenance. It clears EVERY rank, not just residents — and if you are
not the owner, it logs you out too. The confirm says so before it acts.
Only the owner can run it and still be standing afterwards; the
owner's own session is never dropped, whoever runs it.

Accounts are untouched and everyone may reconnect at once, so pair it with
.logins off if you want the house to stay empty. Nothing is persisted by this
command itself.
