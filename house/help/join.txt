.join — accept an invitation

.join on its own answers the invitation you are holding: you arrive
at the inviter's side, wherever they are — through geography and
locked doors alike. Your arrival is announced like any other.

.join <name> does the same and names who you meant. The name must be
the whole of theirs (case does not matter): a near miss is refused
rather than guessed at, and your invitation is left where it is.

You hold one invitation at a time — a new one replaces the last —
so .join always means the most recent. It lapses after five minutes,
or when the inviter moves on; if it has, .join says so and you stay
put.
