.read_report — read the report box

Lists every report filed, newest last, with who filed it and when.
New ones are marked with a *; reading the box clears the "unread
report" notice you get at login.

There is no delete. A report leaves the box only by expiring, after
100 days. That is deliberate: a box its own reader can quietly empty
would not be much of a record.

The box is shared, so reading it marks everything read for everyone
who can see it. Reports are ordinary mail underneath — encrypted at
rest, capped, and aged out by the same machinery.

Who may read this is a permission like any other. By default it is
the owner alone; the operator can widen it.
