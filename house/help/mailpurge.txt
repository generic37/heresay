.mailpurge — delete a whole mailbox unread (owner)

  .mailpurge <name>   their entire box, gone

For a spammed box, and nothing else. It DELETES without decrypting: no one,
at any rank, can read another's mail, and this power is deliberately the
kind that destroys rather than reveals. It does not touch their .revtell
buffer. Logged as a fact — the fact of it, never a word of what was in it.

It asks for YOUR OWN password first, like .deleteuser and .resetpassword.
Someone's letters are gone for good afterwards, and one mistyped line
should not be enough to do that. The password guards the accident, not
the contents — nothing is read either way.

You can only purge the mail of someone below your own rank, and never the
owner's.
